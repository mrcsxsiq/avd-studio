# Code of Conduct

AVD Studio is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where anyone can report a bug, ask a question
or send a patch and be treated with respect.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; most misunderstandings are just that.
- Give and accept feedback about the code, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and people who use the project differently from you.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English, so everyone can follow.

Don't:

- Harass, insult, demean or discriminate against anyone, for any reason.
- Publish other people's private information.
- Troll, derail threads or keep pushing after a decision has been made.

## Scope

This applies to every space of the project: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, the website and any
other channel that represents the project, and to anyone taking part, maintainers included.

## Enforcement

If something crosses the line, report it to the maintainer through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by opening an issue marked "conduct" (it can be a minimal one; details can follow privately). Reports are handled
as confidentially as GitHub allows.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn,
temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does so, in proportion to what happened.
